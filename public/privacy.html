Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how J&T Builders LLC ("J&T Builders", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and request an estimate.

Information we collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide when you fill out our quote form, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Details about the project you are requesting

We also automatically collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and provide a free estimate
  • To contact you about your project and our services
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • To measure and improve our advertising
  • To comply with legal obligations

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate our business, including:

  • Jobber — our customer and job management platform, which powers our quote form and stores your request.
  • Meta (Facebook) — we use the Meta Pixel and Conversions API to measure the performance of our advertising. Data shared with Meta for this purpose is hashed where it identifies you.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

Cookies & tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and support advertising measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing communications, by contacting us at the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted to this page.
